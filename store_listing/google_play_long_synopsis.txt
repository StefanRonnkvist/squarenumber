Square Number is a falling-number puzzle where every placement matters. Guide the active square across a compact five-column board, make matches, and keep the stack from reaching the top.

Find a clear in either of two ways:
- Match at least 2 equal squares touching at an edge or corner.
- Connect at least 3 unique consecutive values orthogonally. Chains can turn corners, but cannot repeat a value.

For each matching group, the score is the sum of its values multiplied by its size. Group scores are added, then multiplied by the cascade multiplier. When cleared squares remove support, falling squares can create another match; the multiplier starts at x1, rises with each clear in the cascade, and resets when the next square appears. Floating markers show points earned.

The pace rises as your score grows. Choose a base fall speed from 0.20x to 1.50x; effective speed increases by 0.10x every 500 points, up to 1.50x. Drag the active square sideways or down and release to snap it to a column. It continues falling until it lands, and landed squares cannot be moved. Pause for a break or start a new run whenever you like. Game over completes the run; Restart begins another.

Set the generated number range and choose system, light, or dark theme. The adaptive interface supports phones, tablets, and desktop screens. Phone gameplay uses portrait orientation; tablet and desktop layouts support portrait and landscape.

A non-empty board is saved when the app moves to the background and restored paused on your next launch. Your highest score and top 10 completed runs stay on your device. A positive current score appears in Score History as Current only when it qualifies for the top 10; Clear History removes completed runs but preserves your highest score. Settings includes Controls, Score History, Information, and Help. Information provides a feedback form with app diagnostics and a hosted-submissions viewer. Sending feedback or loading hosted submissions requires an internet connection. Game progress and scores are stored locally; no account is required.

Return to your stack, chase a new best, and discover the cascade that changes your run.
